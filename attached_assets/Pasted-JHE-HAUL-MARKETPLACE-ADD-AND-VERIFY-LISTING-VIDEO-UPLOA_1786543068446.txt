JHE HAUL MARKETPLACE — ADD AND VERIFY LISTING VIDEO UPLOAD

The Create Listing / Sell flow currently shows the photo upload section, but the listing video upload option is not clearly visible.

Add and verify the marketplace video upload feature.

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:

Each marketplace listing may have:

* Maximum 20 photos
* Maximum 1 video
* Maximum video length: 60 seconds

The video does NOT count toward the 20-photo limit.

==================================================
VIDEO UPLOAD UI

On the Add Photos/Create Listing screen, add a clearly visible section below the photo uploader:

🎥 ADD VIDEO

Display:

“Add 1 video — up to 60 seconds.”

Add a video counter/status such as:

Video: 0 / 1

After a video is selected:

Video: 1 / 1

==================================================
VIDEO ACTIONS

Allow the seller to:

* Select video from phone/device
* Upload video
* Preview video
* Play/pause preview
* Remove video
* Replace video before publishing

Do not allow more than one video per listing.

==================================================
60-SECOND LIMIT

Videos must be 60 seconds or less.

If the seller attempts to select a video longer than 60 seconds, reject it and display:

“Videos must be 60 seconds or less.”

A video exactly 60 seconds long should be allowed.

==================================================
UPLOAD PROGRESS

When uploading a video, show a clear progress state such as:

“Uploading video…”

Do not allow users to accidentally submit the listing while a video upload is incomplete.

Display an understandable error if the upload fails.

==================================================
VIDEO THUMBNAIL

Where technically practical, generate or display a video thumbnail.

Clearly identify video media with a play icon so buyers know it is a video.

==================================================
LISTING DETAIL PAGE

When a listing contains a video:

Display it in the listing media gallery along with the photos.

The buyer should be able to:

* Tap the video
* Play it
* Pause it
* Replay it

Do not autoplay marketplace videos with sound.

Use mobile-friendly video controls.

==================================================
MARKETPLACE CARD

If a listing has a video, optionally display a small video/play indicator on the marketplace listing card.

Do not replace the seller’s chosen cover photo with the video unless specifically designed that way.

The selected photo cover should remain the primary listing card image.

==================================================
VALIDATION

Enforce all limits both client-side and server-side.

Test:

20 photos + no video
→ Allowed

20 photos + 1 video
→ Allowed

1 video under 60 seconds
→ Allowed

1 video exactly 60 seconds
→ Allowed

1 video over 60 seconds
→ Rejected

Second video
→ Rejected

Direct API attempt to upload second video
→ Rejected

Direct API attempt to upload video over 60 seconds
→ Rejected

Unauthorized user attempts to add/remove another seller’s video
→ Rejected

==================================================
FILE SIZE

Use the reasonable video file-size limit previously selected based on the existing DigitalOcean/storage infrastructure.

If no final file-size limit has been selected yet:

Inspect the current storage/upload architecture and choose a practical limit that supports a 60-second marketplace video without creating unnecessary storage or bandwidth costs.

Report the selected file-size limit.

==================================================
DO NOT BREAK

Do not break:

* 20-photo upload
* Photo reordering
* Cover/starred photo
* Listing creation
* Listing editing
* Marketplace browsing
* Messaging
* Offers
* Notifications
* Admin
* JHE Haul delivery
* Authentication

==================================================

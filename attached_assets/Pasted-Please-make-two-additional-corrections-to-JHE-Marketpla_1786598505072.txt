2. Correct the Difference Between “All” and “Items”

Right now All and Items appear to do basically the same thing.

They need clearly different behavior.

ALL

All means ALL Marketplace listings.

It should include:

* Regular merchandise/items
* Vehicles
* Homes/real estate for sale
* Rentals

ITEMS

Items means physical merchandise only.

It should include categories such as:

* Furniture
* Appliances
* Electronics
* Tools
* Auto Parts
* Home & Garden
* Clothing & Accessories
* Restaurant Equipment
* Business Equipment
* Kids & Baby
* Sports & Outdoors
* Collectibles
* Free Items
* Other physical merchandise

Items should NOT include:

* Vehicles
* Homes/real estate
* Apartments
* Houses for rent
* Other rental/property listings

Vehicles should have their own category/filter.

Real estate should be handled through For Sale.

Rental properties should be handled through Rentals.

3. Navigation must remain single-select

Only ONE main marketplace selection should be active at a time.

If I tap:
All → Items

All must immediately deactivate.

If I tap:
Items → Vehicles

Items must deactivate.

If I tap:
Vehicles → For Sale

Vehicles must deactivate.

Do not leave multiple main sections highlighted/open at the same time.

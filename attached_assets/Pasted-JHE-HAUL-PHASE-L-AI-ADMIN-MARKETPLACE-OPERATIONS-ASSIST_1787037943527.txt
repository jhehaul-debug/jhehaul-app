AI ADMIN + MARKETPLACE OPERATIONS ASSISTANT

Phase K is complete and verified.

Now begin Phase L.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT expose Admin AI capabilities to normal buyers, sellers, or haulers.

The assistant must answer using REAL JHE Haul data only.

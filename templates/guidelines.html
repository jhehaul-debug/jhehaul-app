Community Guidelines

Rules that keep JHE Haul Marketplace safe, fair, and useful for everyone.

🔞
18+ Age Requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use JHE Haul Marketplace. By registering and using this platform you certify that you meet this requirement. Accounts found to belong to minors may be removed.

📋 What You Can Sell

JHE Haul Marketplace is for legal, physical goods that can be picked up locally or delivered. Good examples include:

  • Furniture and home goods
  • Appliances and electronics
  • Tools, equipment, and machinery
  • Clothing, accessories, and collectibles
  • Vehicles, trailers, and auto parts
  • Real estate and rental properties
  • Free items and giveaways

Seller responsibility: You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to sell any property you list. Listing items you do not own or have no authority to sell is prohibited.

🚫 Prohibited Items & Behavior

The following are never allowed on JHE Haul Marketplace:

  • Illegal items of any kind
  • Weapons, firearms, or ammunition (unless permitted by applicable law and platform policy)
  • Drugs, controlled substances, or drug paraphernalia
  • Stolen goods or property you do not have the right to sell
  • Counterfeit or fraudulent items
  • Hazardous materials
  • Adult or explicit content
  • Services (this is a physical goods marketplace)
  • Fraud, scams, or misrepresentation of any kind
  • Spam, duplicate listings, or fake listings
  • Harassment, threats, or abusive communication
  • Criminal activity of any kind
  • Suspicious payment requests or phishing attempts

Fraud, scams, criminal activity, and harassment are strictly prohibited. Violations may be reported to law enforcement.

🤝 Be Honest and Respectful

  • Accurate descriptions. List items with honest descriptions including known defects or issues.
  • Real photos. Use your own photos that accurately represent the item — no stock images.
  • Respectful communication. Treat everyone on the platform with courtesy. Harassment and threats are prohibited.
  • Honor commitments. If you agree to a sale, follow through. Don't ghost buyers or sellers.
  • No misrepresentation. Do not misrepresent items, ownership, condition, or your identity.

⚠️ Enforcement

JHE Haul moderators review reported listings and accounts. Violations may result in:

  • A warning to the account holder
  • Removal of the offending listing
  • Temporary suspension of posting privileges
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account ban for serious or repeated violations

JHE Haul does not guarantee every buyer, seller, listing, ownership claim, or transaction. We reserve the right to remove any listing or account at our discretion to protect the community. These guidelines may be updated over time — continued use of the platform means acceptance of the current guidelines.

📧 Questions or Reports

See something that violates these guidelines? Use the Report button on any listing or seller profile, or contact us at jhehaul@gmail.com.

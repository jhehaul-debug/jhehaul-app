🔒 Buying & Selling Safely

Tips to protect yourself and have a great experience on JHE Haul Marketplace.

✅ For Buyers

  • Inspect before you pay. Always check items in person before handing over any money. Photos don't always show everything.
  • Meet in public when possible. A coffee shop, store parking lot, or police department parking area are all good options for small items.
  • Bring a friend for large pickups at a seller's home, especially if you're moving heavy items.
  • Use the built-in messaging system. Keep communication on the platform so there's a record. Be cautious of sellers who quickly push you off-platform.
  • Trust your instincts. If a deal feels off or a seller is pressuring you to act fast, walk away.
  • Don't send money upfront to hold an item to someone you've never met. Agree to meet and inspect first.

✅ For Sellers

  • Be accurate in your listing. Describe condition honestly — include any flaws, damage, or missing parts. Honest listings build trust and prevent disputes.
  • Use real photos. Take your own photos in good lighting. Avoid stock images or photos from the internet.
  • Arrange daytime meetups and let someone know where you're going for large-item pickups at your home.
  • Verify payment before handing over items. Confirm cash or payment has cleared before releasing the item.
  • Don't accept overpayment requests. Scammers sometimes "overpay" and ask for a refund. Refuse and report these.
  • Mark your item sold once a deal is complete so other buyers aren't left waiting.

🚩 Red Flags to Watch For

  • Seller or buyer wants to communicate only by text or email immediately
  • Price is dramatically lower than market value — "too good to be true"
  • Buyer offers to send a check or money order
  • Someone asks you to use gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency
  • Seller can't meet in person and asks to ship with advance payment
  • Pressure to decide or pay immediately

📧 Report a Problem

If you encounter a suspicious listing, unsafe behavior, or anything that violates our community guidelines, please report it immediately using the Report button on the listing page, or contact us directly.

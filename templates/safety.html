🔒 Marketplace Safety

How to protect yourself and have a safe experience on JHE Haul Marketplace.

⚠️
Stay Alert — Fraud, Scams & Criminal Activity Happen

Stay alert for fraud, scams, and criminal activity. Never send passwords, verification codes, banking login information, or other sensitive personal information to another marketplace user.

JHE Haul Marketplace cannot guarantee the identity, honesty, ownership claims, or intentions of every buyer or seller. Use good judgment and report suspicious activity immediately.

🔞
18+ Age Requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use JHE Haul Marketplace. By using this platform you certify that you meet this requirement.

✅ For Buyers

  • Inspect before you pay. Always check items in person before handing over any money.
  • Meet in a public location when appropriate. A coffee shop, store parking lot, or police department parking area are good options for small items.
  • Bring someone for large pickups, especially at unfamiliar locations.
  • Use the built-in messaging. Keep communication on the platform so there is a record. Be cautious of anyone pushing you off-platform immediately.
  • Trust your instincts. If a deal feels wrong or a seller is pressuring you to act fast, walk away.
  • Never send money upfront to hold an item from someone you have never met.
  • Be cautious of unrealistically low prices. If it seems too good to be true, it may be.
  • Never provide another user with your password, verification codes, or banking login credentials.

✅ For Sellers

  • Be accurate in your listing. Describe condition honestly, including defects, damage, or missing parts.
  • Use real photos. Take your own photos in good lighting. Avoid stock images or photos from the internet.
  • Arrange daytime meetups and let someone know where you are for large-item pickups.
  • Verify payment before releasing an item. Confirm payment is received and legitimate before handing over goods.
  • Do not accept overpayment. Scammers sometimes "overpay" and ask for a refund of the difference — refuse and report.
  • Be cautious of unusual payment requests. Gift cards, wire transfers, and cryptocurrency sent by strangers are common scam methods.
  • You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to sell any listed property.

🚩 Red Flags to Watch For

  • Requests for your password, verification code, or banking credentials
  • Requests to move conversation off-platform immediately
  • Price dramatically lower than market value — "too good to be true"
  • Someone asks you to pay with gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency
  • A buyer sends a check or money order
  • Seller cannot meet in person and asks to ship with advance payment
  • Urgency or pressure to decide or pay immediately
  • Suspicious links sent in messages
  • Requests for unusual payment arrangements
  • Claims of escrow services you did not agree to
  • Items that appear to be stolen, counterfeit, or misrepresented
  • Harassment, threats, or intimidating communication
  • Newly created accounts offering high-value items at very low prices
  • Requests for your Social Security number, driver's license, or identity documents

🛑 Common Fraud & Scam Types

Payment Scams
Fake payment confirmations, overpayment tricks, or requests to pay through unusual methods.
Fake Listings
Listings for items that don't exist or don't belong to the seller, often with stolen photos.
Phishing
Links designed to steal your login credentials, financial information, or personal data.
Identity Theft
Attempts to collect your personal information under false pretenses.
Counterfeit Goods
Fake branded items sold as authentic. Selling or knowingly buying counterfeits is illegal.
Stolen Property
Items reported or suspected to be stolen. Buying stolen property can have serious consequences.

📋 How to Report

  • Report a listing: Use the Report Listing button on any listing page.
  • Report a user: Use the Report User option on a seller's profile or listing.
  • Keep important communication on-platform so there is a record available if you need to report suspicious activity.
  • If there is an immediate threat or emergency, contact appropriate emergency services directly. JHE Haul does not replace law enforcement.

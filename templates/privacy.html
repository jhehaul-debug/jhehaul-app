Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What Information We Collect

When you use JHE Haul Marketplace, we may collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo provided when you sign in with Google or GitHub.
  • Listing information — titles, descriptions, prices, photos, and categories you post.
  • Messages — conversations between buyers and sellers on the platform.
  • Phone number — if you choose to add one and opt in to SMS notifications.
  • ZIP code — if you provide one to enable local filtering and distance features.
  • Usage data — pages visited, actions taken, and browser/device information for analytics and improvement.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and display your listings on the marketplace
  • To facilitate messaging between buyers and sellers
  • To send notifications you've opted into (email and/or SMS)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  • To improve the platform based on how it's being used
  • To contact you about your account or transactions when necessary

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share your contact details with other users without your consent.
  • We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

Third-Party Services

JHE Haul uses the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Google OAuth / GitHub OAuth — for sign-in (we receive your name, email, and profile photo).
  • Twilio — for SMS notifications if you opt in.
  • SendGrid — for transactional email notifications.
  • DigitalOcean — for hosting and file storage.
  • Stripe — for any payment processing (card data is handled by Stripe, never stored by us).

Your Choices

  • SMS notifications — opt in or out anytime from your account settings.
  • Email notifications — managed in your account preferences.
  • Delete your account — contact us at jhehaul@gmail.com to request account and data deletion.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. Your session expires when you sign out or after a period of inactivity.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled? Contact us at jhehaul@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of JHE Haul Marketplace constitutes acceptance of the current policy.

How JHE Haul Marketplace Works

Buy and sell locally — simple, fast, and free to list.

📦

Selling is Free

  1. Create a free account with Google or GitHub.
  2. Click + Sell and fill out your listing — title, category, price, condition, and photos.
  3. Your listing goes live and local buyers can find it immediately.
  4. Buyers message you or make an offer directly on your listing page.
  5. Agree on a price, arrange local pickup or request JHE Haul delivery.
  6. Mark your item sold when the deal is done.
🛍️

Buying is Easy

  1. Browse the marketplace or search by keyword, category, or price.
  2. Find something you like — view photos, read the description, and check the condition.
  3. Message the seller directly or make an offer if the listing accepts offers.
  4. Coordinate pickup with the seller at a time that works for both of you.
  5. Need it delivered? Ask the seller about JHE Haul delivery options.

🚛 JHE Haul Delivery — Our Differentiator

Unlike other local marketplaces, JHE Haul can arrange pickup and delivery for large or heavy items. No truck? No problem. Sellers can offer delivery as part of a deal, and buyers can request a JHE Haul pickup directly from the listing page.

This makes JHE Haul Marketplace ideal for furniture, appliances, equipment, and anything too big to fit in a car — without the hassle of coordinating your own transportation.

💬 Messaging

All communication happens through JHE Haul's built-in messaging system. You can ask questions about an item, negotiate price, and confirm pickup details — all in one place, without sharing personal contact information until you're ready.
